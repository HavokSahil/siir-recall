## Editorial choices

These are independently worded study notes, with matched explanation and mathematics sections. They follow the tutorial's proof route and add worked matching-pennies calculations, exercises with results, an incidence-count version of Sperner's parity argument, a mesh estimate, and an explicit justification of radial continuity for polytopes.

The source's product-of-segments example is a square in two dimensions; its p. 8 reference to embedding that square in “R” is treated as a typographical error. Affine coordinates and relative interior are stated explicitly to avoid confusing intrinsic dimension with ambient dimension.

The tutorial's history section distinguishes Nash's 1950 proof using Kakutani from its 1951-based proof using Brouwer. These notes develop the latter route; Kakutani's theorem is not a prerequisite. Historical references listed in the PDF have not been independently consulted.

## Reading order

Read lessons 1–7 for the complete proof. If Brouwer's theorem for compact convex polytopes is already familiar, read lesson 1 and then lessons 6–7. `NOTATION.md` fixes conventions across both panes.
